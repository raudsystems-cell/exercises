Privacy Policy for Raud Fitness

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Raud Fitness ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information Collection and Storage

Your privacy is important to us. Raud Fitness is designed primarily for on-device processing and local storage:

  • Workout & Fitness Data: All your exercise logs, routines, splits, weight, reps, time, and profile settings are stored locally on your device in a secure SQLite database. We do not transmit or sell your personal workout history to external servers.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on user interactions.

Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable data, including:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse location data
  • App performance data, crash logs, and diagnostics

For more information on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, please visit: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions

The App requests only necessary permissions required for core features:

  • Vibration / Haptics: Used for rest timer alert vibrations.
  • Network Access: Used exclusively to retrieve advertisements via Google AdMob.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
